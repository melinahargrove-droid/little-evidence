# Little Evidence · Objective 4 Prototype

A standalone, browser-openable prototype for the Teaching Strategies GOLD Checkpoint Companion concept. This is separate from the Early Eagle Classroom Companion curriculum app.

## Try it

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. Select **Start Movement Check**, move through the three child-facing prompts, finish the child flow, then record teacher observations.

## Prototype scope

- Objective page with the saved Objective 4 progression and Level 6 examples
- Three full-screen movement prompts and a cheerful completion screen
- Teacher-selected observation ratings, consistency, notes, and evidence placeholders
- Teacher-selected developmental indicators, an optional in-between-level suggestion when the next anchor is emerging, editable levels 1–12, next-level evidence, and a family-friendly summary

The child prompts only advance the demo. They do not score physical performance. Prompt ratings are supporting notes only. The level suggestion follows the teacher-selected highest indicator; marking the next even-numbered indicator as emerging suggests the in-between level. This interaction is a prototype, not a validated Teaching Strategies GOLD scoring rule. The teacher makes the final checkpoint decision in GOLD. Photo, video, and voice controls are placeholders.
